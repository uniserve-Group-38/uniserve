# General Platform Rules & Policies
Q: What is Uniserve?
A: Uniserve is a campus services platform tailored for universal convenience. It connects students to peer-providers and local professionals for services like Academics, Tech Support, Cleaning, Food Delivery, Personal Care, Fashion, and Other general errands.

Q: How do items in the shopping cart work?
A: You can book multiple quantities of tangible products or specific package deals. However, for intangible services (like Academics, Tutoring, Consultations, or Barbering), the quantity is strictly limited to 1 per booking in the shopping cart to ensure fair scheduling blocks.

Q: I want to become a service provider. How do I apply?
A: Awesome! Any student or local professional can apply to be a provider. Head over to the "Apply" page (/apply) from the navigation bar. You must submit your business name, a valid email, phone number, relevant experience, operating hours, and clearly select the standard Category that your service falls under.

Q: What happens if a provider cancels my service, or I need a refund?
A: If a provider cancels or fails to deliver the specified service, your payment is 100% refunded to your original payment method within 3–5 business days. If you need to cancel a service, please do so at least 24 hours in advance to receive a full refund. 

# 1. Academics (Tutoring & Education)
Q: What academic services are offered?
A: We have peer-led tutoring for specific college courses, essay review, study strategy sessions, and exam prep. Sessions are typically 60 minutes.

Q: Where do tutoring sessions happen?
A: You and your provider can agree on a location. Most popular spots are the Campus Main Library, Student Union Building, or study rooms in your specific residence hall.

# 2. Tech Support
Q: My laptop won't turn on or has a cracked screen. Can you help?
A: Yes. Our student-led IT support team can run diagnostics. Submit a ticket by booking a Tech Support service, and someone will meet you at the campus library tech desk. We handle virus removal, software installs, hardware diagnostics, and screen repairs.

Q: How long do phone repairs take?
A: Screen and battery repairs for most major smartphone brands take 2-4 hours. Pricing varies by device model but typically starts at $75.

# 3. Cleaning (Chores & Household)
Q: How does the cleaning or laundry service work?
A: Our cleaning category covers everything from dorm room deep-cleans to laundry wash-and-fold. For laundry, you can schedule a pickup through the Uniserve app. We come to your dorm, pick up your clothes, wash, dry, fold them, and return them within 24 hours.

Q: How much does laundry cost and what about delicate items?
A: Standard wash & fold is $1.50 per pound. If you have delicate items, please place them in a separate bag inside your main laundry bag. These items will be air-dried instead of machine-dried to prevent shrinking.

# 4. Food Delivery
Q: What restaurants do you deliver from?
A: We deliver from all catering services on campus, campus cafes, and 15 partnered food establishments on Main Street! 

Q: How late do you deliver food?
A: Student dashers deliver until 2:00 AM on weekdays, and up to 3:00 AM on weekends to fuel your late-night study sessions.

# 5. Personal Care (Grooming)
Q: Where are the grooming services located? Can someone come to my dorm?
A: We partner with several local barbers and salons right off-campus where you can book directly through the app. Alternatively, we have a select group of "Mobile Barbers" who can literally come to your dorm's common area for a fresh cut! 

# 6. Fashion (Tailoring & Style)
Q: My pants are too long, do you do alterations?
A: Absolutely! The Fashion category includes talented design students who offer hemming, tailoring, custom embroidery, and styling advice. Typical turnaround time for a hem is 48 hours.

# Other / Errands
Q: Can I hire someone to pick up something from the store or mail a package?
A: Yes, book a service under the "Other" or errands category. Student runners can grab groceries from the local mart, pick up pharmacy prescriptions, or drop off your returns at the post office.
